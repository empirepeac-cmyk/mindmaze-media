# Don't step in the ring 🍄 — prompts

## Story (strengthened)
Old folklore: never step inside a ring of mushrooms ("fairy ring"). Whoever steps in leaves our world.

1. **Wonder (0–8s):** A beautiful young woman walks barefoot through a magical glowing forest: giant bioluminescent mushrooms, fireflies, floating pollen, soft blue and gold light. She is happy and amazed. She finds a perfect circle of small glowing mushrooms and smiles.
2. **The step (8–10s):** She steps inside the ring. Silence. Every mushroom in the ring goes out at the same moment.
3. **The burn (10–18s):** From under her foot the world starts to burn away like paper: glowing orange edges, curling ash, embers floating up. Behind the burnt "paper" is the real forest: dead black trees, fog, cold grey moonlight. The colours die, the fireflies turn to ash.
4. **The shape (18–28s):** In front of her there is a black shape in the fog, darker than the dark. The camera tilts up slowly. It keeps going up: a figure 4 metres tall, thin, made of shadow and dead branches. At the top, a long pale face with no eyes slowly tilts down toward her.
5. **Ending:** Freeze + "Would you step in? YES or NO" + "PART 2 at 100 comments".

Hook on frame 0 (I add it in the edit): a 0.5s flash of the face, then "Never step inside a mushroom ring."
